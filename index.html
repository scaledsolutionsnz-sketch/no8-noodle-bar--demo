The dishes

What Queenstown keeps ordering

The plates our regulars come back for, and the ones people name most often in our Google reviews.

Mapo tofu 麻婆豆腐

Silken tofu in a chilli bean sauce with Sichuan pepper and spring onion, served with steamed rice. Numbing, not just hot.

Chilli oil hand-pulled noodles 油泼面

Wide noodles pulled by hand, then finished with hot chilli oil, crushed peanuts and greens. The bowl people photograph.

Beef noodle soup 牛肉面

Sliced beef and hand-pulled noodles in bone broth that starts early in the morning. The bowl regulars order without looking.

Fried noodles 炒饭

Wok-fried noodles with pork, cabbage and carrot, tossed hot and fast so the edges catch. Big enough to share, usually not shared.

Cumin lamb 孜然羊肉

Lamb through the wok with cumin, chilli and spring onion. Dry-fried rather than saucy, built to eat alongside rice.

Pork mince noodles 杂酱面

Noodles under slow-cooked pork mince with shredded cabbage and peanuts. Stir it through before the first mouthful.

About us

A proper Chinese kitchen in Frankton

No.8 Noodle Bar sits inside Queenstown Central, and every plate leaves the kitchen the way it would back home, colour, heat and aroma, not a toned-down version of it. Noodles are worked by hand, the beef bone broth goes on early, and the shared dishes come out big enough for the whole table.

Pamela and the team look after the room seven days a week, from a quick bowl on the way through Frankton to a long table of shared plates on a Friday night. Bookings by phone, and takeaway if you would rather eat at the lake.

  • Noodles and dumplings made by hand daily
  • Dine in or takeaway, 10am to 10pm, seven days
  • Regulars from Frankton, Queenstown and the whole basin
