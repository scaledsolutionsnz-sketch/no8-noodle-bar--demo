The menu

Noodles, dumplings and Sichuan plates

What we cook at Queenstown Central, grouped the way people order it. Dine in or takeaway, seven days, 10am to 10pm.

Hand-made noodle soups

手工面 · 汤面
  • Braised beef noodle soup Beef bone broth, hand-pulled noodles, braised beef.
  • BBQ pork noodle soup Char-grilled pork over noodles in clear broth.
  • Wonton noodle soup Pork wontons folded in-house.
  • Oil-splashed noodles Hot chilli oil poured over fresh noodles and spring onion.
  • Dan dan noodles Sesame, chilli and minced pork, tossed at the table.

Dumplings & small plates

锅贴 · 小菜
  • Fried dumplings — pork Pan-fried until the bases turn golden.
  • Fried dumplings — vegetarian The one our reviewers keep naming.
  • Wonton soup A lighter start, or a small bowl on its own.
  • Barbecued pork bun Steamed, soft, filled with sweet char siu.
  • Stir-fried cabbage Wok-blistered with garlic and a little chilli.

Sichuan specials

川菜
  • Sichuan boiled fish 水煮鱼 Fish fillets under dried chilli and Sichuan pepper in chilli oil.
  • Sichuan boiled beef 水煮牛肉 Thin beef in the same numbing chilli-oil broth.
  • Grilled fish hotplate A whole fish brought to the table on the hotplate.
  • Mapo tofu Silken tofu, fermented bean and Sichuan pepper.
  • Chilli-fried chicken Diced chicken with dried chilli, leek and sesame.

From the wok

热炒
  • Sweet & sour pork Crisp pork, glossy sauce, onion and pineapple.
  • Beef with onion Sliced beef in a rich brown sauce.
  • Braised duck Slow-braised, sliced, served with its braising liquor.
  • Chinese BBQ lamb ribs Cumin, chilli and sesame, eaten with your hands.

Rice

饭
  • Signature fried rice Wok-fried with egg and spring onion, served in a wooden barrel.
  • BBQ pork on rice Char-grilled pork, jasmine rice, greens and gravy.
  • Steamed jasmine rice By the bowl, for the table.

Prices and daily specials

Prices and specials change with the seasons and what is coming in, so we keep them on the board in the restaurant rather than online. Ring the kitchen on +64 3 428 2723 and we will talk you through it — or ask about a set menu for a group of eight or more.
