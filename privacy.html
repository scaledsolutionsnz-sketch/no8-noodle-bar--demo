Privacy policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. Who we are

No.8 Noodle Bar 绽放.中餐馆, Shop A12/19 Grant Road, Frankton, Queenstown 9300, New Zealand. This policy explains how we handle personal information, in line with the Privacy Act 2020.

2. What we collect

This website has no forms of any kind, so we collect nothing from you simply by browsing it. We only hold personal information when you choose to give it to us:

  • By phone — your name, phone number and booking details when you reserve a table.
  • By email — your name, email address and whatever you write to us about a group booking or an enquiry.
  • In the restaurant — nothing beyond what is needed to take and serve your order.

3. How we use it

Only to take and manage your booking, answer your enquiry, and contact you if something about your booking changes. We do not sell personal information, and we do not add you to a marketing list without asking.

4. Payments

Card payments are handled by our EFTPOS and payment providers in the restaurant. We do not see or store your full card number.

5. Third-party services on this site

  • Google Maps — the map near the bottom of the homepage is embedded from Google and loads only when you scroll to it. Google may set cookies and collect usage data under its own privacy policy.
  • Google Fonts — typefaces are served by Google.
  • Google Reviews — the review buttons take you to Google, where anything you post is public and governed by Google's terms.
  • Social media — our Facebook, Instagram and TikTok links take you to those platforms, each with its own privacy policy.

6. How long we keep it

Booking notes are kept only as long as we need them to run the restaurant. Emails are kept in our mailbox unless you ask us to delete them.

7. Your rights

Under the Privacy Act 2020 you may ask what personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Ring us and we will sort it out.

8. Cookies

We do not set advertising or tracking cookies ourselves. Embedded Google services may set their own — you can block or clear cookies in your browser settings.

9. Children

This site is not aimed at children and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

10. Contact

Privacy questions: call +64 3 428 2723, or use the email button in our contact section.
